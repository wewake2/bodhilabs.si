01 · Structure

Unstructured in. Model-ready out.

Clinical notes, scanned forms, radiology archives, contracts. We turn documents nobody could query into datasets a model can train on, with protected health information removed by fine-tuned models, not by people reading pages.

  • Fine-tuned extraction and de-identification models, trained on your document types
  • PDF, scanned image, DICOM, and free-text sources
  • 500M+ documents processed; 1.5 PB in one engagement, delivered in 3.5 months
  • Output in the schema you specify, ready for training, analytics, or licensing

02 · Resolve

One record per person, across every system.

Your customers and patients exist in five systems under five slightly different names. We resolve them into one identity graph, without a commercial CDP or a team doing it by hand.

  • Commerce: Shopify, point-of-sale, Google Analytics, Meta, payment gateways
  • Healthcare: radiology systems, LIMS, EMRs
  • Probabilistic and deterministic matching, tuned to your tolerance for false merges
  • Delivered as a maintained pipeline, not a one-time export

03 · Prove

Evaluation that holds up to scrutiny.

A dataset is only useful if you can show it meets the protocol. A model, only if you can show where it's right. We build the evaluation platforms that answer both, for you, your reviewers, or your regulator.

  • Dataset evals: inclusion and exclusion criteria, de-identification verification, coverage
  • Model evals: clinical next-step prediction, task-level scoring, reinforcement-learning environments
  • Federated analytics: evaluate across institutions without moving the data
  • Every delivery ships with its own eval
